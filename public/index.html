WebXR Meetup

Walk up to people, connect, and earn bonus points for every interest you share.

Interests (pick up to 3)

How to play
  • Desktop: WASD / arrows to move, drag to look, C to connect, 1–4 emotes, Enter to chat.
  • Headset: press the goggles icon (bottom right). Point at the floor and pull the trigger to teleport. Point at a person and pull the trigger to connect. A/X waves, B/Y connects with the nearest person.
  • Each new connection is worth 10 points, plus 5 for every interest you share.
