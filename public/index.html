WebXR Meetup

Walk up to people, connect, and earn bonus points for every interest you share.

Interests (pick up to 3)

How to play
  • Desktop: WASD / arrows to move, drag to look, C to connect, 1–4 emotes, Enter to chat.
  • Headset: press the goggles icon (bottom right). Point at the floor and pull the trigger to teleport. Point at a person and pull the trigger to connect. A/X waves, B/Y connects with the nearest person.
  • Each new connection is worth 10 points, plus 5 for every interest you share.
  • UNO: in the Game Dev Room, walk up to a chair at the card table and press E (or click / trigger the chair). When 2–8 seated players press ✋ Play, everyone gets 8 cards. Click or trigger a card to play it. Use 🂠 Draw (F) when you can't play, and hit UNO! (U) when you're down to one card, or when someone else is, before they do. Leave any time with Q or 🚪 Leave.
  • Every interest has its own room. Walk into a coloured booth to teleport there, and use the gold 🏠 booth to get back to the Main Plaza.
